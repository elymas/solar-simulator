# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Halley's Comet (핼리 혜성) — a selectable body on a genuinely eccentric orbit that dives from beyond the outermost dwarf planet to well inside Mercury and back, trailing a tail that always streams directly away from the sun and grows brighter and longer as it nears perihelion. The orbit keeps real Halley's eccentricity (0.967) and retrograde inclination (162.3°); only its size and period are display-scaled — 700 display units and 7.6 simulation years instead of 76 — so a child watching at ordinary speeds actually sees a perihelion pass rather than waiting hours for one. It appears under a new "혜성" divider in the planet list and on the mobile strip, with Korean kid facts and a spoken narration on selection. (SPEC-EVENTS-001)
- Asteroid belt and Kuiper belt — two rock fields rendered as instanced meshes: a dense band between Mars and Jupiter, and a sparser, thicker, slower ring out beyond Neptune. Individual rocks drift at speeds derived from their distance from the sun, so each ring shears over time rather than turning like a solid disc. The rocks are scenery and are deliberately not tappable in the 3D view — dragging or tapping across a belt never selects a rock — but "소행성대" and "카이퍼 벨트" appear in the planet list and mobile strip under a new "띠" divider, and selecting one frames the ring with the camera and reads out its Korean facts. On devices that fall behind their frame budget the belts thin out first, before the app touches sun glow, level of detail, or resolution; devices that boot into the constrained quality tier start with the thinner fields. (SPEC-EVENTS-001)
- Planetary alignment celebration — when four or more planets gather within a 30° wedge of sky as seen from the sun, a banner reading "행성들이 줄을 섰어요!" appears once with a spoken callout, then dismisses itself. It will not fire again until the planets drift apart past 40°, so a formation hovering on the edge cannot make the banner flicker. The banner is announced to screen readers (`aria-live="polite"`) and presents without animation when the system asks for reduced motion. (SPEC-EVENTS-001)
- Meteor shower annual events (`src/utils/meteorData.js`, `src/effects/MeteorShower.js`) — Four canonical showers (Quadrantids, Lyrids, Perseids, Geminids) with Korean names are rendered as shooting-star streaks on the night-side upper dome, with spawn rate scaled by intensity function (peaks at the peak date, tapers to 0 at range edges). HUD shows a Korean notice `${koreanName}가 쏟아져요!` and speaks the notice via the shared TTS channel when the shower range is entered, re-arming only on exit. The 'meteors' degrade step joins the Earth-view budget ladder immediately after 'aurora', so frame-budget pressure sheds streaks before bloom effects. Reduced-motion respected; pool pre-allocated (12 full, 6 constrained tier per SPEC-MOBILE-001). (SPEC-EARTH-003)
- International Space Station marker (`src/utils/issOrbit.js`, `src/earth/ISSMarker.js`) — Small emissive sprite orbiting the Earth with a 92-minute sim-time period and 51.6° inclination on a symbolically circular orbit (no TLE, no live tracking). Position is a pure function of sim time, unit-testable. Marker is tappable (included in raycast target set) to present Korean kid facts and speak them via the shared TTS channel. HUD toggle (defaulting ON) controls visibility and is synced with the existing aircraft-layer toggle pattern. (SPEC-EARTH-003)
- Worldwide aircraft snapshot (`.github/workflows/flights.yml`, `src/utils/constants.js` FLIGHT_DEFAULTS) — The Earth view's aircraft come from a scheduled snapshot covering the whole planet, replacing the Seoul/Incheon point query that replaced London. There is no query centre any more, so `lat`/`lon`/`radiusNm` are gone from FLIGHT_DEFAULTS and no status line names a city. The LIVE/LOADING/OFFLINE/RATE_LIMITED state machine and the empty-sky vs error distinction remain unchanged from SPEC-EARTH-002. (SPEC-EARTH-003)
- Size comparison ("크기 비교", `src/play/SizeCompare.js`) — a new button in the info panel's kid view opens a lineup that answers "how big IS it?" by counting: the selected body's disc is drawn one lane wide, and the reference body (Earth, or the Sun) is drawn as that many little discs laid side by side, so the picture and the sentence make the same claim. Ratios are computed from the real `radius` values in `planetData.js`, never from the symbolic display radii. Counts round to the nearest half and any row that cannot be stated within ±10% of the true ratio is dropped rather than fudged, so bodies with no honest comparison (e.g. Sirius A at 1.71 suns) simply do not offer the button. The sentence is spoken through the shared Korean TTS channel. (SPEC-PLAY-001)
- Rocket trip ("로켓 발사", `src/play/RocketTrip.js`, `src/play/RocketTripScene.js`) — opens an overlay showing the launch world, the destination, a dashed road between them and a rocket driving it, then speaks a destination-specific Korean travel fact from a data-driven table (`src/play/travelFacts.js`) together with the distance. Distances are computed from `planetData.js`'s own AU figures as the gap between the two orbits — the closest the worlds ever come — and the Moon, orbiting Earth, states its own orbit radius and is worded differently for it. The picture is explicitly **not** to scale and carries the eclipse diagram's "그림 크기는 실제와 달라요" note, because 크기 비교 owns the size claim; the durations are real approximate travel times on one shared fast-cruise basis, so they rise with distance. A 🔊 button replays the narration. Selecting another body or leaving the view closes it. (SPEC-PLAY-001)
- Celebration effects and sound (`src/effects/Celebration.js`, `src/audio/sfx.js`) — arriving at a newly selected body plays a brief pooled sparkle burst with a chime; tapping a star plays a distinct twinkle. Sounds are synthesized through a small Web Audio wrapper that sits beside the existing TTS module and is governed by the **same** persisted "소리" toggle, so one switch silences speech and effects together; the audio context is unlocked from the first selection gesture for iOS. Particles come from a fixed 160-particle pool that is reused across bursts and hidden entirely when at rest. (SPEC-PLAY-001)
- Daily missions and sticker book (`src/play/missions.js`, `src/play/stickers.js`, `src/play/StickerBook.js`) — three Korean play prompts ("고리가 있는 행성을 찾아보세요!") are chosen per real calendar date by a deterministic rotation, so the same day always shows the same missions. Completing one plays a fanfare, a sparkle burst at the body that completed it, and spoken praise, then awards a sticker exactly once. Stickers persist in `localStorage` under a single namespaced key and survive reload; the sticker book overlay shows earned stickers vivid and unearned ones as grayed silhouettes, and carries the day's mission list with a done/total badge. Corrupt or unavailable storage degrades to an in-memory session instead of crashing. (SPEC-PLAY-001)
- Mobile planet icon strip (`src/ui/PlanetStrip.js`) — a bottom, horizontally-scrollable strip of tappable body icons that becomes the PRIMARY body selector at viewport widths ≤768px, positioned directly above the time-controls bar. Selection is synced bidirectionally with 3D-scene taps and the desktop sidebar: selecting anywhere highlights the strip item and scrolls it into view, and tapping the strip drives the same select/focus path as a 3D tap. Item list is generated from the shared body registry, so bodies added by later SPECs appear automatically. (SPEC-MOBILE-001)

### Changed

- Render quality tier is now decided from observed device signals (`devicePixelRatio`, `hardwareConcurrency`, `deviceMemory`) via the new `decideQualityTier` function in `src/utils/quality.js`, instead of a user-agent mobile check. High-end phones (e.g. iPhone 17 Pro-class) now boot at `pixelRatio = min(devicePixelRatio, 2)` with full bloom instead of being force-capped to `pixelRatio` 1. The conservative static cap (`pixelRatio` 1 + texture cap + LOD upgrades disabled) still applies only where `hardwareConcurrency <= 4` AND `deviceMemory` is available and `<= 4`; the existing `FrameBudgetDegrader` (`src/utils/performance.js`, untouched) remains the dynamic safety net for devices that report no memory signal. (SPEC-MOBILE-001)
- Kid-sized tap targets: planet-list rows are now ≥48px tall, all buttons (play/reset, list-toggle, HUD, mute) are ≥44×44px, and the list collapse caret is ≥32px, across `src/ui/PlanetList.js`, `src/ui/TimeControls.js`, and the mobile media query in `index.html`. (SPEC-MOBILE-001)

### Fixed

- Live aircraft returned after the provider died. `api.airplanes.live`'s keyless v2 endpoint began answering 403, so the Earth view showed no aircraft at all. The browser cannot simply call another feed — none of the keyless ADS-B services send a CORS header — and a Cloudflare Worker proxy cannot reach them either: adsb.fi and OpenSky both sit behind Cloudflare, which answers a Worker's subrequest with 403 (WAF) or 522. A scheduled GitHub Action now fetches OpenSky's whole-planet query from a runner, trims ~9,200 state vectors to 800 records of six fields (~72 KB, ~15 KB gzipped), and force-pushes them to an orphan `flight-data` branch that raw.githubusercontent.com serves with `access-control-allow-origin: *`. (SPEC-EARTH-002, SPEC-EARTH-003)
- The flight HUD no longer claims to be live. GitHub throttles the `*/5` cron hard — observed gaps of 30-45 minutes — so the status line dropped "실시간", reads its age from the snapshot's own timestamp rather than the fetch clock, and states minutes past the first minute instead of "2070초 전". (SPEC-EARTH-003)
- The rocket journey was invisible on a phone. It used to be drawn into the shared scene and framed with the shared camera, both of which sit behind the info panel that covers a phone's whole viewport — the button appeared to do nothing. It is an overlay now. (SPEC-PLAY-001)
- The info panel's content no longer starts underneath the floating ☰ and 🏆 toggles on mobile, which were slicing the planet thumbnail in half. Content begins below them; the toggles stay tappable so the sticker book is reachable without closing the planet first. (SPEC-MOBILE-001)
- Touch selection no longer fires on `touchstart`. A body is now selected only when a single-finger touch ends having moved no more than 8px (`TOUCH_TAP_MAX_DRAG_PX`) from its start point (`src/controls/InteractionManager.js`), mirroring the existing mouse drag-guard pattern. Starting an orbit drag on a body no longer selects and camera-focuses it; a qualifying tap on empty space while a body is selected now deselects, matching mouse behavior. (SPEC-MOBILE-001)
- `TimeControls` buttons (`src/ui/TimeControls.js`) no longer shrink below their declared 44×44px size when the bar's content overflows at phone widths: `.control-btn` is pinned with `flex-shrink: 0`, and `.time-controls` wraps to a second row (mobile-only, scoped to `@media (max-width: 768px)`) instead of squeezing its children. Found in a real-browser (non-jsdom) verification pass, since jsdom's lack of a layout engine cannot reproduce flex-shrink defects. Fixes a follow-on desktop-only regression where an unscoped wrap caused the bar to get stuck at two rows after a resize-down-then-up round trip (shrink-to-fit width feedback loop above the 768px breakpoint).

## Known References

Entries above are attributed to the SPEC that introduced them: SPEC-MOBILE-001 — `.moai/specs/SPEC-MOBILE-001/`; SPEC-EVENTS-001 — `.moai/specs/SPEC-EVENTS-001/`; SPEC-EARTH-003 — `.moai/specs/SPEC-EARTH-003/`; SPEC-PLAY-001 — `.moai/specs/SPEC-PLAY-001/`.

The rocket (SPEC-PLAY-001) does not fly to Halley's Comet (SPEC-EVENTS-001). Its travel-fact table speaks one fast-cruise duration per body, and at e=0.967 the comet has no single one-way distance to quote — so it is excluded structurally, exactly as the Sun and the stars are, and simply offers no launch button.

The SPEC-PLAY-001 entries have not yet had their manual device pass (audio unlock on real Safari/iOS, celebration feel, sticker-book touch targets, reduced-motion sweep, native-Korean read-aloud). Four known presentation follow-ups are recorded in `.moai/specs/SPEC-PLAY-001/progress.md` §E.2 (M6) rather than here, since they describe deferred work rather than shipped behavior.
